Dynamic robot artwork and animation engine adapted from the local Pulse project.
Source: Pulse/Sources/Pulse/Panel/BotMark and Pulse/Sources/Pulse/Resources/bot-data.json.
Pulse's Swift runtime is supplied under Apache License 2.0; see BotMark-LICENSE.txt.
The geometry and behavior data in bot-data.json originate from iduu/grokbot-animation,
an unofficial study of x.ai/bot. Pulse's Docs/decisions/bot-mark-geometry.md explicitly
records that these reference-derived assets have no open-source redistribution grant.
The Apache license above does not grant rights to those assets.
Upstream references: https://github.com/iduu/grokbot-animation and https://x.ai/bot
Modifications: Codenotch integration, resource validation, localized controls, AppKit rendering and visibility-driven 60 Hz scheduling.
